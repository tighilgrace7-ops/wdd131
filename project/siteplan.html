Tighil's Haven

A Place Where Flowers Tell Beautiful Stories

Site Name

Tighil's Haven

I chose the name Tighil's Haven because it gives the website a personal identity while creating a feeling of peace, beauty, and warmth. It reflects the beauty of flowers and provides a welcoming place where visitors can learn about flower, their meanings, arrangements, and how to care for them.

Site Purpose

Tighil's Haven is an informational website created to help visitors discover the beauty and meaning of different flowers. The website provides information about flower types, their meanings, beautiful flower arrangements, and suitable flowers for different occasions. It also shares helpful flower care tips to guide visitors on how to keep their flowers fresh and beautiful.

Scenarios

Scenario 1

What flowers are suitable for birthdays, weddings, and other special occasions?

Scenario 2

what do different flowers symbolize, and what meanings do they communicate when given as gifts?

Scenario 3

How can I care for my flowers to keep them fresh and beautiful for longer?

Color Schema

The color schema for Tighil's Haven will use soft floral colors to create a warm, elegant, and welcoming appearance.

  • Blush Pink (#F7B8C8): Used for decorative accents, highlights, and selected design elements.
  • Warm Cream (#FFF9F2): Used as the main background color.
  • Botanical Green (#356B4A): Used for headings, navigation, and important design elements.
  • Deep Charcoal (#3F3538): Used for paragraphs and general body text.

Typography

Tighil's Haven will use two fonts to create a clean, elegant, and easy-to-read design.

  • Roboto: Used for paragraphs and general body text to make the website comfortable and easy to read.
