Site Name

Tighil's Haven

I chose the name Tighil's Haven because it gives the website a personal identity while creating a feeling of peace, beauty, and warmth. It reflects the beauty of flowers and provides a welcoming place where visitors can learn about flower, their meanings, arrangements, and how to care for them.

Site Purpose

Tighil's Haven is an informational website created to help visitors discover the beauty and meaning of different flowers. The website provides information about flower types, their meanings, beautiful flower arrangements, and suitable flowers for different occasions. It also shares helpful flower care tips to guide visitors on how to keep their flowers fresh and beautiful.
